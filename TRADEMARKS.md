# AI-HPP Trademark Policy

## Purpose

The open licenses for this repository grant copyright permissions. They do not
grant rights to use the AI-HPP name, logos, badges, or certification marks in a
way that suggests endorsement, assessment, conformity, or certification.

This policy is separate from the copyright licenses and applies to uses of
AI-HPP identifiers as trademarks, service marks, or certification marks.

## Permitted referential use

You may use the plain-text name **AI-HPP** truthfully to:

- identify this project or link to this repository;
- describe compatibility with, implementation of, or modification of a
  particular published version of the AI-HPP Standard;
- state that your work is derived from AI-HPP material, with the attribution
  required by the applicable copyright license; or
- discuss, review, teach, or report on AI-HPP.

Referential use must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, official status,
assessment, or certification. Modified standards must be clearly identified as
modified and must not be presented as official AI-HPP publications.

## Restricted claims and marks

Without prior written authorization, you may not:

- use **AI-HPP Certified**, **AI-HPP Conformant**, **AI-HPP Assessed**, or a
  confusingly similar claim as a product, service, or organization designation;
- use an AI-HPP logo, badge, seal, or certification mark;
- state or imply that AI-HPP or its maintainers have approved, tested,
  assessed, certified, or endorsed a system, organization, person, or service;
- register or seek rights in a name, domain, social-media identifier, company
  name, product name, or mark that is confusingly similar to an AI-HPP mark; or
- sublicense or authorize another party to use an AI-HPP mark.

Publishing evidence of an implementation or self-assessment does not by itself
create authorization to use a restricted claim or mark.

## Authorization and enforcement

Permission to use a restricted mark requires a separate written agreement from
the relevant mark owner. Any authorization may define scope, duration,
surveillance, quality-control, and termination requirements.

Questions and permission requests should be submitted through the repository's
maintainer contact channel. Report suspected misleading uses through the same
channel.

Nothing in this policy limits uses permitted by applicable law, including
nominative fair use, or grants rights that the policy issuer does not own. This
policy may be revised as the assurance and certification program develops.
